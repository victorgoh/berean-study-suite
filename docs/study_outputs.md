# Study Outputs Reference Guide

For pastors, theologians, and biblical scholars, having instant access to editable, permanent files is essential. Unlike standard chat assistants where research disappears when the session ends or gets buried in temporary artifact caches, the **Berean Study Suite** automatically writes all theological output directly to physical, editable markdown (`.md`) documents, images, and Word documents inside your local workspace.

This guide details where those files are saved, how they are named, and how to utilize them in your sermon preparation or academic research.

---

## 📁 Output Directory Structure

All outputs generated by the Berean agents and skills are organized under three main directories at the root of your workspace:

```
workspace-root/
├── berean/                  # All theological analyses, outlines, sermons, and devotions
│   ├── berean_YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS_desc.md          # Standalone study manuscripts
│   └── berean_YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS_study_folder/    # Multi-phase orchestrated studies
│       ├── 000-request_and_study_plan.md
│       ├── 001-data_retrieval.md
│       ├── 002-exegesis.md
│       ├── 003-theology.md
│       ├── 004-application.md
│       ├── 005-pre_final_overview.md
│       └── 006-final_response.md
├── images/                  # All generated biblical illustrations and presentation slides
│   └── YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS_description.jpg
└── export/                  # Exported Word documents (.docx)
    └── docx/
        └── YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS_document.docx
```

## ✍️ How Outputs Are Saved

The saving behavior depends on whether you generate a standalone study manuscript, run an orchestrated multi-phase research pipeline, or produce visual aids and Word exports.

### 1. Standalone Study Manuscripts
When you request a single topic, sermon outline, devotional meditation, or run a composite study pack (such as `devotional_study_pack`, `sermon_study_pack`, `passage_exegesis_pack`, or `covenant_theology_pack`), the agent formulates the deliverable and saves a standalone Markdown file directly into the `berean/` directory.

* **Format**: `berean/berean_YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS_description.md`
* **Examples**:
  - `berean/berean_2026-08-29-01-56-00_john_15_abide_in_me_devotion.md`
  - `berean/berean_2026-08-29-02-08-00_silence_and_solitude_study_questions.md`
* **Benefit**: Unique timestamp prefixes ensure files never overwrite each other, allowing you to compare different translation notes, commentaries, or devotional drafts side-by-side.

### 2. Multi-Phase Orchestrated Studies (`/berean` & `/berean-plus`)
When executing a full multi-phase study via `/berean` or `/berean-plus`, a dedicated timestamped folder is created to group all related research files into sequential phases.

* **Format**: `berean/berean_YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS_study_title/`
* **Structure**: Each phase is saved as a discrete step (e.g., `000-request_and_study_plan.md`, `001-data_retrieval.md`, `002-exegesis.md`, `003-theology.md`, `004-application.md`, `005-pre_final_overview.md`, and `006-final_response.md`).
* **Benefit**: Maintains a complete, verifiable paper trail of scripture retrievals, original language exegesis, commentary citations, and covenantal synthesis.

### 3. AI Biblical Illustrations (`/image`)
When generating illustrations for sermon slides, bulletin covers, or study guides:

* **Format**: `images/YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS_description.jpg` (or `.png`)
* **Examples**:
  - `images/2026-08-29-01-47-00_moses_parting_red_sea.jpg`
  - `images/2026-08-29-01-53-50_john_15_abide_in_me.jpg`
  - `images/2026-08-29-02-05-57_jesus_and_zacchaeus_in_tree.jpg`
* **Benefit**: High-resolution, widescreen (16:9) artwork ready for presentation software (ProPresenter, PowerPoint, Keynote) or digital publications.

### 4. Word Document Export (`/docx`)
To convert any Markdown study output into a styled Microsoft Word document:

```bash
/docx berean/berean_2026-08-29-01-56-00_john_15_abide_in_me_devotion.md
```

* **Format**: `export/docx/YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS_description.docx`
* **Benefit**: Formats Markdown headers, blockquotes, and tables into clean Word styling suitable for immediate printing or sharing with church teams.

## 🛠️ Benefits for Pastoral & Scholarly Workflows

1. **Fully Editable Text**: All study manuscripts are saved in Standard Markdown (`.md`). You can open them in any text editor, IDE (Antigravity, Cursor, VS Code), or Markdown note-taking app (such as Obsidian) to modify outlines, add personal annotations, or integrate with study vaults.
2. **Local-First & Offline Access**: All files are written directly to your local drive, ensuring instant offline access with zero reliance on cloud connections.
3. **One-Command Word Conversion (`/docx`)**: Instantly bridge the gap between AI markdown research and professional church document printing.
